To earn back $250,000 from one machine, it would have to sell 43 jars a day, every day, for two years.

So the plan grows the two things that can scale: the number of hosts, and the size of each order.

For scale: Sprinkles' cupcake ATMs held about 760 cupcakes and sold 500 to 1,000 a day on Beverly Hills foot traffic (Fortune, 2014). The chain closed every store in December 2025.
