    Our story

    A life without cupcakes is a life without love.

    Michelle Wainwright left the pharmaceutical world to bake. Ten years later Cute as a Cupcake is a Merrillville storefront, a pink truck, a dessert cart, a line of jars that ship nationwide, and a nonprofit, Cutie Cares, that fights food insecurity at home.

    2015A kiosk at Southlake Mall and the farmers markets.
    2016The storefront opens on US Route 30.
    2020The shop closes for the pandemic. The jar keeps the business alive.
    2025Wins the Chicago Sky Foundation pitch. Machines roll out.
